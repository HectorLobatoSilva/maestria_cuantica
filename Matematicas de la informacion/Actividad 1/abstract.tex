\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This document presents a review and analysis of the fundamental concepts of the mathematical theory of communication proposed by Claude E. Shannon in his historic 1948 paper \parencite{shannon1948mathematical}. It addresses the PCM and PPM modulation methods, the justifications for using a logarithmic measure for information, and the intrinsic relationship between entropy and channel capacity to efficiently encode messages.
